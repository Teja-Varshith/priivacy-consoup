This Privacy Policy explains how Consoup ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Consoup mobile application.

Consoup is an educational application designed to make the Indian Constitution easier to understand and explore. The application provides access to constitutional information, learning features, article explanations, reading progress, bookmarks, and related educational content.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in to Consoup using Google Sign-In, authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication.

We may collect and store:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo
  • Firebase user ID

Learning and App Data

To provide learning and personalization features, Consoup may store basic application activity associated with your account, including:

  • Articles or constitutional content you have read
  • Learning progress
  • Bookmarks or saved content
  • Quiz or learning activity where applicable
  • Other basic preferences required for the application

This information is stored using Firebase Firestore.

Technical Information

We may collect limited technical and diagnostic information to maintain and improve the application, including:

  • Device and operating system information
  • Application usage information
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • Application performance information

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics may process this information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate users through Google Sign-In
  • Maintain your Consoup account
  • Save your learning progress
  • Remember bookmarks and saved content
  • Provide educational and learning features
  • Understand how the application is used
  • Improve application performance and reliability
  • Identify and fix crashes and technical problems
  • Maintain platform security and prevent misuse

3. Data Storage and Security

Consoup uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Firebase Firestore, to securely process and store applicable user information.

Communication between the application and its backend services is protected using encrypted connections such as HTTPS. We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Third-Party Services

Consoup uses trusted third-party services to provide and improve the application. These may include:

  • Firebase Authentication – account authentication and sign-in
  • Firebase Firestore – storing account and learning data
  • Firebase Analytics – understanding application usage
  • Firebase Crashlytics – crash reporting and diagnostics
  • Google AdMob – displaying advertisements

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

5. Advertising

Consoup may display advertisements through Google AdMob.

Because Consoup is intended for a broad audience, including younger users, advertising is configured to follow applicable Google Play and Google Families requirements for child-directed content.

We do not knowingly request or encourage children to provide personal information for advertising purposes.

Advertising providers may process limited information necessary to serve and measure advertisements in accordance with their applicable policies.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers only when necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or improve Consoup, including the third-party services described in this Privacy Policy.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the application.

7. User Data Control

Users can:

  • Access their Consoup account
  • Update information associated with their account where supported
  • Manage saved content and learning activity within the application
  • Request deletion of their Consoup account and associated personal data

Uninstalling the application does not automatically delete information already stored on our servers. Users who want their account and associated data deleted should follow the account deletion process below.

8. Children's Privacy

Consoup is designed to be accessible to a broad audience and may be used by children aged 3 years and above.

We aim to minimize the collection of personal information and only collect information necessary to provide the application's functionality.

Consoup does not knowingly ask children to provide unnecessary personal information. Advertising and other third-party services are configured, where applicable, to comply with applicable child-directed and Google Play Families requirements.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information that should be deleted may contact us using the contact information provided below.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Consoup, our services, or applicable legal and platform requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page. The updated effective date will be shown at the top of the Privacy Policy.
